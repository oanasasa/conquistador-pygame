# Conquistador – Game Rules

A quiz-driven territory game on a grid of 12 regions.

## Rules (v1)

1. Up to 3 players share one keyboard. A question appears, and each player has a buzz key.
2. The first to buzz in answers with keys 1-3. A wrong answer locks that player out of this question.
3. The correct answerer picks a region and claims it, even if another player owns it.
4. The next question starts, and the cycle continues until one player owns the whole map.
